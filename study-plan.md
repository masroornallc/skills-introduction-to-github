## 📌 Key Rules to Prevent Forgetting (Your Biggest Problem)

You mentioned you lose focus and forget fast. Here is what works:

1. **Study in 45-minute blocks with a 10-minute break** (Pomodoro technique). Never study for more than 90 minutes without a 15-minute break.

2. **Write before you read** — Before studying a chapter, write down in 2 minutes everything you already know (or think you know) about the topic. This primes your brain.

3. **Recall, don't re-read** — After finishing a topic, close the book and write down what you just learned from memory. This forces active recall and dramatically improves retention.

4. **One formula, one example** — For every formula, write one worked numerical example. You remember the example faster than the formula itself.

5. **Morning review ritual** — Spend the first 10 minutes of each study session reviewing flashcards and formulas from the previous 3 days.

6. **Teach it back** — Explain a concept out loud as if teaching it to someone. If you cannot explain it simply, you do not know it well enough.
